2X Cash — Economy Mod for Euro Truck Simulator 2

Doubles job payouts and driver revenue. Experience (XP) stays at normal game values.

What changes:
- Job and cargo market revenue (2x)
- Hired driver revenue (2x)
- Hired drivers always return with cargo

Use only one mod from this family at a time.

Works with the current ETS2 version and map DLCs.
Does not change fines, garage prices, or fuel costs.

Author: Drago - Fabio Monreal
