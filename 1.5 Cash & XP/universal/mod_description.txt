1.5 Cash & XP — Economy Mod for Euro Truck Simulator 2

A lighter boost: 1.5x job payouts and 1.5x experience gain.

What changes:
- Job, cargo market, and driver revenue (1.5x)
- Delivery / free roam / parking XP bonuses (1.5x)

Use only one mod from this family at a time.

Works with the current ETS2 version and map DLCs.
Does not change fines, garage prices, or fuel costs.
