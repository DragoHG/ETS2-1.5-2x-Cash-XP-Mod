2X XP — Economy Mod for Euro Truck Simulator 2

Doubles experience gain from deliveries, free roam, road discovery, and manual parking. Job payouts stay at normal game values.

What changes:
- Delivery / free roam / road discovery XP (2x)
- Manual parking bonuses, including double trailers and loading parking (2x)

Use only one mod from this family at a time.

Works with the current ETS2 version and map DLCs.
Does not change fines, garage prices, or fuel costs.

Author: Drago - Fabio Monreal
